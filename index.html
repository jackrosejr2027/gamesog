About

Card Arcade 1.0 Final · 25 Oct 2026

An offline arcade with 94 games and 28 interactive Card Tricks routines: solitaire, solo adventures, board games, seven solo puzzles with 1,000 numbered levels each, nine additional world puzzles with 100 levels each, Gin Rummy, and two original fantasy card duels. Ordinary the Scattering has a 226,400-record catalog and 52 starter decks; PullCrest has 150 five-form creature families and 21,256 printings. Procedural catalog variants share mechanics and are not independently balanced. Card Tricks includes mathematical tricks and clearly labelled chance experiments.

Credits

  • The Long Road is after Ashley’s solo card RPG at roboheartbeat.com.
  • The Quiet Year-style Journal, Savage Worlds-style Skirmish and Castle Falkenstein-style Challenges are unofficial, unaffiliated games inspired by The Quiet Year, Savage Worlds and Castle Falkenstein. They are not made, endorsed or supported by the publishers of those games.
