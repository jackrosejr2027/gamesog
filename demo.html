About

Card Arcade 1.0 Final · 25 Oct 2026

An offline arcade with 75 games and 28 interactive Card Tricks routines: 32 solitaire games, 25 solo adventures, Checkers, Chinese Checkers, Mancala, Mahjong Solitaire, Card Memory, Bowling Solitaire, Stack Showdown and ticket games. New RPG adventures are original short games. Card Tricks includes mathematical tricks and clearly labelled chance experiments.

Credits

  • The Long Road is after Ashley’s solo card RPG at roboheartbeat.com.
  • The Quiet Year-style Journal, Savage Worlds-style Skirmish and Castle Falkenstein-style Challenges are unofficial, unaffiliated games inspired by The Quiet Year, Savage Worlds and Castle Falkenstein. They are not made, endorsed or supported by the publishers of those games.

Release notes

1.0 Final · 25 Oct 2026

  • Games resume when switching through the arcade menu or previous/next arrows. New game remains an explicit reset.
  • Mobile headers, touch controls, board scrolling and card-duel layouts adapt to narrow and short screens.
  • Complete local assets and catalogs are included in offline installation and the refreshed downloadable ZIP.

1.3.0 · 25 Oct 2026

  • Stack Showdown: new one-card sideline mode with recall and swapping.
  • Checkers, Chinese Checkers and Mancala against computer opponents.
  • Mahjong Solitaire with 1,000 seeded, solution-verified layouts, hints and undo.
  • Detailed rules, controls and game-specific strategy for every game and all 28 Card Tricks routines.
  • Logic fixes for Klondike moves, completed-game guards, wild-Ace evaluation, the final journal entry, Card Tricks timer cancellation and several RPG resource rules.
  • Stack Showdown levels now begin at 1; older zero-based saves migrate safely.

1.2.0 · 25 Oct 2026

  • Twenty new Card Tricks routines: encoding, error correction, shuffle invariants, arithmetic, secret communication and interactive predictions. Chance experiments are labelled honestly.
  • Twenty original card RPG adventures with separate objectives, actions and in-game rules.
  • Twenty more solitaire games, including Yukon, Russian, Forty Thieves, Canfield, Scorpion, Simple Simon, Aces Up, Bisley, Flower Garden and Gaps.
  • Optional dungeon background for all RPG games; preference is saved and included in Save / Load.
  • Expansion modules and dungeon artwork are included in offline caching.

1.1.0 · 25 Oct 2026

  • New arcade hub with Solitaire, Card Puzzles, Solo Adventures, Card Tricks and ticket games.
  • Added Spider, Pyramid, Golf, TriPeaks, Accordion, Clock, Monte Carlo, Bowling, Beleaguered Castle, Calculation, La Belle Lucie and Card Memory.
  • In-game rules describe the variants, controls and win conditions.
  • Stack Showdown keeps its own level selection, shop and saved progress.

1.0.0 · 25 Oct 2026

  • Stack Showdown levels, with a shop for Peek, Redraw, +3 boosts and side-game tickets
  • Side games: High-Low Streak and Lucky Flip
  • More Games: Scoundrel, Klondike Solitaire, FreeCell, The Long Road, Savage Worlds-style Skirmish, The Quiet Year-style Journal and Castle Falkenstein-style Challenges
  • Card Tricks with eight tricks
