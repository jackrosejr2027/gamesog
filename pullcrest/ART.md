# Illustration Provenance

150 family portraits were generated for this project through Websim image generation without uploaded reference images. Six original elemental atlas prompts requested unfamiliar anatomy and fantasy field-guide illustrations, explicitly excluding existing franchises, text and logos. Individual portraits were cropped with Pillow, borders/text removed where practical, and saved as genuine 256 by 224 WEBP files.

Files art/family-000.webp through art/family-149.webp correspond to the family IDs in creatures.json. All five forms in a family share the portrait with stage tinting. These are 150 family illustrations, not 750 separately painted evolutions or 17,825 separate pictures. Species names and mechanical families are original procedural combinations; illustrations are evocative portraits rather than a scientifically exact depiction of each evolution stage.
